# Human Outreach Style Patterns

## Pattern 1: Specific opener

Incorrect:
`I wanted to reach out because your brand seems like a great fit for partnership opportunities.`

Correct:
`Saw your team just expanded the hydration line and thought of two endurance athletes in our roster who already post around race-week fueling.`

## Pattern 2: One idea per paragraph

Incorrect:
`We work with many creators and offer full-service support, strategic consulting, and premium activations that drive measurable growth across channels.`

Correct:
`We can test one creator-led pilot around your next launch. If it performs, we expand.`

## Pattern 3: Grounded CTA

Incorrect:
`Let me know your thoughts and we can discuss next steps at your earliest convenience.`

Correct:
`Open to a 15-minute call next week to see if this is worth testing?`

## Pattern 4: Remove template filler

Avoid:
- "I hope this message finds you well"
- "I wanted to take a moment to"
- "In today's fast-paced world"

Replace with direct context in sentence one.
